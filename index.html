Every visit

What to expect

Here's how a visit goes, from the moment you sit down to the moment you leave.

  1. The consult

    Tell me what you want, or let me guide you. I'll look at your hair pattern and tell you what works before the clippers come on.

  2. The cut

    Clean fades, sharp lines and a razor finish with enhancement. I take my time and check every angle. No rushing to the next head.

  3. The finish

    Hot towel and face massage on Platinum, plus the 24k gold facial on Platinum Plus. You leave right, from every angle.
